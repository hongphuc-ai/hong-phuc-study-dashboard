HOW THE AGENT THINKS

Better sorting starts with careful observation.

The AI agent does not guess an item’s materials or your local collection rules. It uses the details you provide, then points out what still needs checking.

01 / IDENTIFY

Identify the item

The agent considers materials, cleanliness, labels, and attached parts. If a photo or description leaves gaps, it asks instead of making a claim.

02 / SEPARATE

Separate materials

It suggests separating different materials and considering safe reuse before disposal.

03 / YOU DECIDE

Check before sorting

Review the suggestion against local guidance. The agent will not discard, list, or schedule a pickup for you.

HELPFUL DETAILS TO START

Start with what you can see.

No specialist vocabulary needed. Describe the item’s labels, cleanliness, and any parts joined together.

◎

Materials and labels

Paper, plastic, metal, glass, or symbols printed on packaging.

◷

Item condition

Is it clean or food-stained, dry or broken, made of one material or several?

✎

Local collection area

Your city or the collection guidance you want to check.

PRINCIPLES & LIMITS

A recycling label is only part of the story.

Packaging symbols can help identify materials, but collection options depend on local services and the item’s condition.

✳ Evidence-led suggestions

  • Use the described material, labels, and condition; call out uncertainty.
  • A label alone does not guarantee an item is recyclable locally.
  • If coatings, mixed materials, or residue are unclear, the agent recommends checking further.

⌁ Your decision

  • No location lookup, contact with collectors, or pickup bookings.
  • No improvised handling advice for hazards such as damaged batteries or leaking chemicals.
  • Check local guidance before sorting.
